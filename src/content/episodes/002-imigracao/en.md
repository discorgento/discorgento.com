In the second episode of the discorgento podcast, Jonatan Machado and Mary talk with **Rafael Corrêa Gomes** and Tchays, a couple from Sertãozinho, Brazil, who live in Montreal. Rafael started as a webmaster at a print shop that built PHP websites, studied systems analysis and development, and is now technology director at a company in Montreal. Tchays is a digital product designer who has worked in e-commerce and briefly studied civil engineering before committing to a design career.

The couple lived about a year and a half in Miami before arriving in Canada in January 2020 on a work visa. They walk through the Quebec program, which requires a job offer and later French, one year of local experience and culture courses for permanent residence. They describe their first day (ID number, bank account and a visit to the company), the covid lockdown in their second week, and why it matters to have savings and every document translated. They also share tips on the -20 °C winter, missing family and everyday safety.

On the professional side, Rafael describes a technology director's routine, balancing team, processes and clients, and argues that **headless** only makes sense with complex architecture, unique requirements and an experienced team. He also discusses Magento's future at Adobe and recalls building a Correios shipping extension for Chrome that reached 500 merchants, while the couple talks about communication, languages and trusting your own decisions.
