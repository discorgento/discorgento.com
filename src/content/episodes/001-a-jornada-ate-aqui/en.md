The first episode of the Discorgento podcast is a conversation between its three hosts about **the journey so far**. Ivan Augusto has worked with Magento for about six years, since Magento 1.8, and recalls that his first internship was at a health insurance company, where he single-handedly rewrote a legacy C# desktop system as a web application. Jonatan Machado is about 30, has been programming for around 15 years and has worked with Magento for about 10, after also using PrestaShop and WooCommerce. Mary Ferro used to work at a compounding pharmacy, studies Systems Analysis and Development and had been a junior developer for seven months.

A large part of the talk is about **anxiety** and self-pressure early in a career. The hosts talk about exercise, hobbies such as riding motorcycles and gaming, the weight of the work environment and the importance of stopping and resting when a problem will not budge. Mary says she pressures herself to learn fast precisely because she has someone with 15 years of experience by her side.

In the final part, the conversation turns to the future. Ivan talks about the challenges of headless projects, where the back end stays on Magento and the front end uses another technology, and the hosts discuss why maintenance weighs more than development and why moving from Magento to Shopify can mean rebuilding features. Machado wants to become fluent in English and study React, Next.js and Shopify Hydrogen; Mary wants to focus on front-end work and English. In the advice to their past selves, they mention taking more risks, leaving the comfort zone and going slow and steady.
