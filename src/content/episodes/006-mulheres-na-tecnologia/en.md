In this episode, without Machado, Mary Ferro brings together three guests to talk about **women in tech**. Rosana Macena, 34, lives in the countryside of southern Minas Gerais, has a degree in Business Administration and studies Internet Systems; she started as an intern alongside Mary, configuring Magento stores, and now works with Angular. Larissa Lira, 26, is a software engineer who started as a Magento front-end developer. Catarina, 28, studied Information Systems on a scholarship, began as a design intern and is now at her third tech company.

How each of them got in is the core of the conversation. Larissa worked in telemarketing, collected recyclables and was a cashier before winning a scholarship at Facebook's Estação Hack; she studied on her own for eight months, lost a freelance gig paying R$50 per piece and was told it "wasn't for her" before breaking into the field. Mary left a compounding pharmacy after Alura's Imersão Dev and sold her book and manga collection to buy a laptop. Rosana went through a career mentorship program and is now a tutor in it.

The theme running through everything is **anxiety**: the pressure to deliver on the same day, the fear of asking for help and the feeling of being "the junior of the juniors". Larissa says she once juggled three contracts and more than 16 hours of work a day, and that the turning point came when she started trusting her own work, including when negotiating salary. At the end, each of them talks about plans, such as English fluency, running her own business and motherhood, and the advice she would give her past self.
